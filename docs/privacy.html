Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026. This policy describes Lenvra Wealth Lab version 0.1.0 as it is published. It is maintained by the app’s publisher and is updated when the app’s data practices change, including before optional iCloud sync or in-app purchases are enabled.

Who maintains the app

Lenvra Wealth Lab is maintained by Sameer Chavan. General support is available through the support page. Privacy questions can be raised through the support page.

Information on your device

The app stores the scenarios, names, notes, goals, journal entries, immutable revisions, reviews, reading-completion records, preferences and onboarding choices that you enter. Journal text and review history remain in the local database. Calculations run on your device. There is no Lenvra account, bank or brokerage connection, advertising SDK, behavioral tracking SDK or developer-operated upload service in this version.

Learning and search

Educational articles are bundled with the app for offline reading. If you choose Mark as read, the app stores the article identifier, content version, English locale and date locally. Visits and scrolling do not automatically record completion. You can remove a version’s completion after confirmation; annual review totals may change. Workspace searches run on your device across saved scenarios, goals, journal history, reviews and articles. The app does not save search history or send search terms to a search service. Choosing a further-reading link opens an external source in your browser; that website follows its own privacy practices. There are no embedded websites or automatic article downloads.

Optional reminders

The app supports opt-in local reminders for journal reviews and weekly goal check-ins. They are off by default. Enabling them requires your device notification permission. Alerts contain generic wording, not investment names, amounts or journal text. There is no remote push service or push-token collection. Settings → Notifications lets you disable them. The app reconciles its notification queue after saved-data changes and when opened; interrupted changes are reconciled at next launch. Your system notification and Focus settings control presentation and delivery.

Optional Home Screen widgets

Weekly educational insights use bundled content without personal data. Personal widget sharing is off by default. If enabled in Settings → Widgets, a protected local file shared only with the Lenvra widget extension contains selected goal and scenario names, their identifiers, a saved starting-progress ratio and a due-review count. It excludes journal wording and financial amounts. Privacy lock prevents personal details in new snapshots. Turning sharing off requests a refresh, but an already displayed image may remain until iOS refreshes it. Snapshots older than one day are rejected when read. This cache is excluded from device backups.

Backups and cloud services

App-enabled iCloud synchronization is currently off. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your system settings. Optional iCloud sync and in-app purchases are not active in this version. If either is enabled in a later version, this policy will describe its behaviour before that version ships.

Exports and imports

When you choose to export or share, a file may contain your financial assumptions, scenario names, notes, journal text, revisions and reviews. Exported files are not encrypted by Lenvra. Anyone who can access a file can read it. Your selected destination may have its own privacy practices. Importing a supported archive adds copies of scenarios, goals and journal history. Reading completions instead merge by article version, keeping the earliest saved date; repeated imports do not duplicate those marks.

Annual review PDFs contain activity counts, confidence and emotional-context summaries, intended holding-period summaries and saved tags, with report dates and counting limits. They exclude Journal prose, but tag names may still contain private information. Export is initiated by you through the native Files destination picker.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete the relevant items, reset the workspace or remove the app. Settings → Your data provides confirmed local deletion. This does not delete exported files or independently retained Apple backups. Manage those through the destination service or your Apple settings.

Security

The app uses iOS file protection for its local database. No security measure eliminates all risk. Device access and the destinations you choose for exported files affect confidentiality. The app includes an optional workspace lock using Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, with immediate, one-minute or five-minute auto-lock after leaving the app. Fresh launches require authentication when the lock is enabled. App-switcher content is hidden by default and always hidden with the lock enabled. This controls app access; it does not independently encrypt exports.

Support and this website

This documentation is hosted by GitHub. GitHub processes website requests and public issue submissions under its own privacy statement. These pages contain no analytics scripts or third-party embeds. Never post private financial data in public support issues.

Policy changes

This policy is updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date above marks the current version. Material changes will be reflected here before the version that makes them is released.
