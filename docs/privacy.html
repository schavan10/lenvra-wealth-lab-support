Privacy policy — draft

Draft for review — not a release-approved policy. Last updated September 20, 2026. This describes the current development build. It must be reviewed and updated before release and before optional cloud or purchases ship.

Who maintains the app

Lenvra Wealth Lab is maintained by Sameer Chavan. General support is available through the support page. A private contact channel and any jurisdiction-specific rights process must be completed before this policy is finalized.

Information on your device

The current development app stores the scenarios, names, notes, goals, journal entries, immutable revisions, reviews, preferences and onboarding choices that you enter. Journal text and review history remain in the local database. Calculations run on your device. There is no Lenvra account, bank or brokerage connection, advertising SDK, behavioral tracking SDK or developer-operated upload service in the current build.

Learning and search

Educational articles are bundled with the app for offline reading. Workspace searches run on your device across saved scenarios, goals, journal history, reviews and articles. The app does not save search history or send search terms to a search service. Choosing a further-reading link opens an external source in your browser; that website follows its own privacy practices. There are no embedded websites or automatic article downloads.

Backups and cloud services

App-enabled iCloud synchronization is currently off. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your system settings. Optional iCloud sync and StoreKit purchases are planned but not active in the current build. Their actual behavior and applicable Apple services must be documented here before they are enabled in a release.

Exports and imports

When you choose to export or share, a file may contain your financial assumptions, scenario names, notes, journal text, revisions and reviews. Exported files are not encrypted by Lenvra. Anyone who can access a file can read it. Your selected destination may have its own privacy practices. Importing a supported archive adds copies and does not replace existing records.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete the relevant items, reset the workspace or remove the app. Settings → Your data provides confirmed local deletion in the current development build. This does not delete exported files or independently retained Apple backups. Manage those through the destination service or your Apple settings.

Security

The app uses iOS file protection for its local database. No security measure eliminates all risk. Device access and the destinations you choose for exported files affect confidentiality. The development build includes an optional workspace lock using Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, with immediate, one-minute or five-minute auto-lock after leaving the app. Fresh launches require authentication when the lock is enabled. App-switcher content is hidden by default and always hidden with the lock enabled. This controls app access; it does not independently encrypt exports. Real-device verification remains required before release.

Support and this website

This documentation is hosted by GitHub. GitHub processes website requests and public issue submissions under its own privacy statement. These pages contain no analytics scripts or third-party embeds. Never post private financial data in public support issues.

Policy changes

This draft will be updated as features and data practices change. It does not claim attorney approval. The final policy, effective date, contact process and App Store privacy declarations remain release requirements.

Maintained by Sameer Chavan. No analytics or tracking scripts on these pages.
