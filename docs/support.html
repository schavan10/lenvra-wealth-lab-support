Support

Lenvra Wealth Lab is maintained by Sameer Chavan. The app is currently in development.

Annual review PDF

In Journal, open Annual review, choose the year and select Export annual review. The report includes retained activity counts, original confidence and emotional-context summaries, intended holding periods and your saved tags. Goals are a current snapshot. The report explains its dates and counting limits. Journal prose is excluded, but tags can contain private information. Review the notice before choosing a Files destination; exported copies are not encrypted by Lenvra.

Reading completion

Open an article in Learn and choose Mark as read to keep a local, version-specific reading record. Opening an article does not mark it complete. Remove this completion clears the selected version after confirmation. Completion records are included in JSON backups and local reset; repeated imports merge the earliest completion date. Annual review counts each topic once in a year, even if multiple versions were marked.

Report a problem

Open a support issue on GitHub. Include your app version, iOS version, device model and the steps that led to the problem. A GitHub account is needed to post.

Issues are public. Do not include financial amounts, journal text, account details, backups, export files or unredacted screenshots. This channel is for general support only. A private contact channel is not yet configured; do not disclose sensitive information here.

Where is my data?

Current builds keep scenarios, goals, journal entries, revision/review history and preferences in the app's local storage. The developer cannot retrieve a lost local database. Apple backups follow your device settings.

Export or delete

The current development build provides Settings → Your data for JSON export, additive import and confirmed local deletion. Export a backup before deleting data or removing the app if you want to keep it. Exported files outside the app must be deleted separately.

Learn and search

The Learn tab contains 17 offline educational topics. Search the library there, or use Search on Home to find saved scenarios, goals, journal revisions, reviews and articles. A journal search result opens the version containing the matching words. Further-reading links require a browser and internet access; the article itself remains available offline.

Local reminders

Settings → Notifications provides optional journal-review reminders and weekly goal check-ins. Journal alerts are grouped at 9 a.m. on future review days; goal check-ins use Sunday mornings when a goal is saved. The app schedules up to 48 upcoming alerts, with up to eight weeks of goal check-ins, and refreshes when opened. Past review days remain in the app without catch-up alerts. Check system notification and Focus settings if alerts are silent. Delivery and device verification remain part of release testing.

Home Screen widgets

The development build includes Goal progress, Saved scenario, Journal review and Weekly insight widgets in small and medium sizes. Add them through the Home Screen widget gallery. Weekly insights need no sharing permission. Other details remain private until you enable sharing and select records in Settings → Widgets. Progress uses saved inputs, not live balances. iOS controls refresh timing, so changes may not appear immediately. Privacy lock hides personal details from new snapshots; previously displayed images can remain until refreshed. Device and appearance verification is still in progress.
