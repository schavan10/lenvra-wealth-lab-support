Disclosures

Effective October 19, 2026. These disclosures describe Lenvra Wealth Lab version 0.1.0 and match the Disclosures screen inside the app.

Educational purpose

Lenvra Wealth Lab is an educational tool. It provides hypothetical calculations and personal planning worksheets. It does not provide investment, tax, accounting or legal advice, and nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security or to adopt any particular financial strategy.

Illustrations are not predictions

Every figure the app produces is a deterministic arithmetic illustration of the assumptions you enter. Returns, inflation, fees, contributions and time horizons are values you choose, including the example values the app starts with, which are illustrative only and editable. Results are not forecasts, projections of your actual outcome, or guarantees. Real investment returns vary, can be negative, and are affected by taxes, costs and events the app does not model.

What the app does not do

Wealth Lab does not execute trades, hold or transfer money, connect to bank or brokerage accounts, read your holdings, or recommend securities or allocations. It has no Lenvra account, no advertising or analytics code, and it does not send your entries anywhere. Assumptions, goals and journal entries you record are your own statements of intent, not advice given to you.

Your own decisions

You are responsible for your financial decisions. Consider consulting a qualified financial, tax or legal professional about your particular circumstances before acting on anything you explore in this app.

Currency and scope

Amounts are in United States dollars. Changing your device region does not convert values. The app does not model taxes, transaction costs, product-specific fees beyond the fee assumption you enter, or jurisdiction-specific rules.

Publisher

Lenvra Wealth Lab is published by Sameer Chavan. Questions can be raised through the support page. These disclosures are updated when the app’s behaviour changes.
